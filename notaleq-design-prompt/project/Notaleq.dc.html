DIRECTION

Calm · precise · trustworthy

It is a money tool, so the surface stays quiet and the numbers do the talking. A refined, Samsung-inspired numpad sits a notch above the stock OS feel — heavier keys, a clear depressed state, restrained motion.

WHY DEEP TEAL

One accent, with intent

Teal reads as money and balance without the alarm of green-vs-red accounting. It carries the brand, the active line, and the primary commit key — and never competes with the error and negative cues.

WHAT TO NOTICE
  • Active line is big. The figure being typed dwarfs the settled rows, with a blinking caret.
  • Total sits above the pad, never scrolls away, and never collides with the OS keyboard — the pad is custom.
  • Error line is flagged (tint + glyph + “مُستبعد”) and excluded — total stays 1,750.
  • A key is depressed (0) — inset shadow + 1px down, with implied haptic + click.
